This game mode quizzes similar to classic.  The question shows on the screen and everyone answers at once.

Each turn, a random player gets to be the driver.

When the question is asked on the phones and the host computer, before anyone gets to see the answer options for the question, the driver is asked how confident they are.  They have a lever that they can drag to select 3 different options: "Not confident", "Confident", "Extremely confident". They pull the lever to the option that they want then lock in the answer by holding down a button that says "Go!".

Then the answer options will be given, and each player can select the answer they think is correct.

Not confident selected:
* If at least 1/4 of the players get the answer correct, no speed is gained but no fail points are given.
* If under 1/4 of the players get the answer correct then give one fail point.
Confident selected:
* If at least 2/3 of the players get the answer correct, 20 mph is gained and no fail points are given.
* If between 1/4 and 2/3 get the answer correct only one fail point is given.
* If under 1/4 get the answer correct, 2 fail points are given
Extremely confident selected:
* If all players get the answer correct, 40 mph is added and no fail points are given.
* If between 2/3 and 99% (with at least one person getting the answer wrong) get the answer correct only one fail point is given.
* If between 1/2 and 2/3 are correct, give two fail points.
* If under 1/2 give three fail points.

For each fail point, the car looses half its speed, so if the car is going 60 mph and one fail it would now be going 30 mph.  two fails and it would be going 15 mph, etc.

If the car goes under 20 mph, the car has crashed and the speed goes to 0.  If the car is crashed, there is no driver, so skip the confidence selector.  Once they group of players answer a question with at least 1/2 correct, the car has been repaired and is back on the road at 30 mph.

For distance calculations, assume that each turn takes 10 minutes.

## Display
The graphics will be a retro purple, blue and orange pixel art style.  The game will be a side scroller, so the car will be driving right to left with the camera fixed on the car while the background moves past the camera.  The background will be a city skyline that moves slowly and closer palm trees that zip by.  The faster that the car is moving, the faster the background will zip by.  The car is a Porsche 911.

Above 200 mph the car takes off into space: the road and background slide down off the screen, and stars streak across instead. Each star leaves a trail that fades over time; the faster the car goes, the longer the trail lasts. When the car drops back to 200 mph or less, the background slides back up from the bottom and lands with a thud.

On each reveal, every player who got the question wrong (or didn't answer) is thrown out of the back of the car: on the road their avatar tumbles out and bounces away down the road behind it; in space it drifts off backwards, spinning slowly, with no bounce. The projector shows everyone who missed; each phone shows only that player's own avatar.

## Sound
The sound will be low fidelity arcade replica.  The music will be similar arcade racing game.  The music tempo will increase with the speed of the car.

## Decisions
The game ends after every question in the quiz has been asked once.  The result is the total distance driven.

The car starts at 30 mph.

Everyone drives once before anyone drives again.

The driver has 20 seconds to choose.  After that the lever is locked where it is.  The same happens if the driver disconnects.

A player who does not answer counts as wrong.

Each turn adds 10 minutes of driving at the speed the car has after that turn.

A turn while the car is crashed has no driver.

There are awards for "Most correct answers" and "Best driver" (most mph gained while driving).

Pacing is Manual or Auto-advance, the same as in Classic.
