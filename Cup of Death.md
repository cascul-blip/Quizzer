On the projector, there is a round table.  Each avatar of a player is sitting at the table.  There are 3 cups in front of each avatar (1 to 6, set in the lobby).

On the phones, you only see your cups on the screen.

### Quiz mode
When entering quiz mode, each player has a 6 sided dice roll across their screen.  The dice roll is random for each player, 1-6.  They have to answer the number that was generated on the dice correctly before a 30 second timer runs out.  Once they get the number of questions correct within 30 seconds, they stop and wait for the others to finish.

There is a red timer bar that is on the top of the screen of both the projector and the phones that gets smaller as the time fades away.

If the player gets enough questions correct, they are safe and do not have to drink one of the drinks in front of them.

If the player does not get enough questions correct, they have to choose which cup they have to drink.

### Drink Mode
There are cups of bubbling liquid in front of the player (3 by default).  Only 1 of them is poisonous.  They have to select a cup to drink.

If the player chooses a cup that is not poisonous, then they go back to quiz mode.
If the player chooses a cup that is poisonous, then they die and are out of the game, they can still participate in answering questions on their own to learn.

On the host screen, you see the cups in front of them that are remaining.  If a player dies, then the avatar for that player is removed.

After the drink mode completes, it goes back to the quiz mode.  When going back to the quiz mode, there are 2 seconds less than the quiz mode time last time.

## Environment
Inside a western saloon.  The table that the players are at is a round wooden table.

The sound is western, with twangs and string music.  Think about the good, bad and ugly main theme.
## Decisions
Each player starts with 3 cups by default; the host can pick 1 to 6 with the "Cups" drop-down in the lobby. A cup that turns out to be safe is gone, so the chance of poison goes (with 3 cups) 1 in 3, then 1 in 2, and the last cup is always poisoned (it's drunk without choosing). The poisoned cup is picked at random each time a player drinks.

The quiz timer starts at 30 seconds and is 2 seconds shorter each round, down to 10 seconds. The quiz ends early once every living player is safe.

A wrong answer just moves on to the next question (the lobby's "Protect from abuse" still slows down players who keep guessing).

Everyone who has to drink picks a cup at the same time, with 10 seconds on the red bar. Anyone who hasn't picked by then (or is disconnected) gets a random cup. The projector then reveals the drinkers one at a time: they lift the cup, gulp, and survive or die.

The last player standing wins. If everyone left dies in the same round, they share the win. Questions repeat until the game ends.

While the quiz is on, the projector shows each player's die and progress (e.g. 2/5) above their head, and SAFE once they reach it.

The music is original, inspired by the spaghetti-western sound: a whistled melody, twangy guitar and galloping hooves, getting more intense as the timer runs out, with a tense heartbeat loop while the cups are picked and drunk.

There are awards for "Most correct answers", "Luckiest drinker" (most drinks survived), "Never thirsty" (most rounds safe) and "Unluckiest" (poisoned by their first cup, earliest).
