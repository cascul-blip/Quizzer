A two-act game. In the first act the whole class works together to defeat a dragon that is sleeping on a pile of gold. When the dragon falls, its hoard spills across the cave and the class splits into teams that race each other to grab as much gold as they can.

This is the first mode that starts cooperative and turns competitive. Submarine Squad and Racer are whole-class co-op all the way through and have no winner inside the class; Dragon's Hoard has a co-op win (slaying the dragon) followed by a team winner.

## Lobby
Choose **Game mode → 🐉 Dragon's Hoard**.

Settings:
* **Teams** (2–6, default 2, but never more teams than players).  A team of 1 is normal: with 3 players and 3 teams, everyone is their own team.  The teams are shown in the lobby the same way as Land Grab and Tallest Tower, and the host can drag a name to another team.  During Act 1 the teams don't matter; they only show up at the transition.  If only one player is in the game, there is one team, and Grab chests pay as Coins instead.
* **Dragon toughness**: Easy, Normal, Hard (default Normal).  This sets how many hits the dragon takes per player (see Act 1).
* **Battle time**: 3, 5 or 7 minutes (default 5).  The time limit for Act 1.
* **Fire breath**: every 30, 45 or 60 seconds (default 45).
* **Scramble time**: 2, 3, 4 or 5 minutes (default 3).  The length of Act 2.
* **Protect from abuse** (the existing checkbox, on by default).

## Act 1: Slay the Dragon (co-op)
The projector shows a huge dragon curled around a mountain of gold in a cave.  Above it is one big health bar that the whole class shares.

* Everyone answers questions on their own phone at their own pace, the same as Tallest Tower.  Questions come in a random order and repeat once they run out.
* **Every correct answer is 1 hit on the dragon.**  The player's avatar flashes in at the bottom of the projector with a small sword slash, and the health bar goes down.
* A wrong answer does nothing to the dragon, and the correct answer is shown on the phone (the same ✓/✗ screen as the other self-paced modes).
* Dragon health = **players × hits per player**: Easy 6, Normal 10, Hard 15 (at least 30 health in total).  Players who join late add their hits to the dragon's health.
* The dragon has three stages: healthy, wounded (under 66%), and badly wounded (under 33%).  Each stage has its own art.  At every new stage the dragon roars and **fire breath comes 10 seconds sooner** (but never sooner than every 20 seconds).

### Fire Breath (Shield of Faith)
On the Fire breath timer, the dragon rears back and breathes in.  The projector and every phone show a warning for 3 seconds: **"The dragon is about to breathe fire! Raise the Shield of Faith!"**

* Everyone's self-paced question is paused, and **the whole class gets the same question at once**: the **Shield of Faith** (Ephesians 6:16, the shield that quenches the fiery darts).
* The Shield of Faith question is **the question the class has gotten wrong the most so far in this game** (the same tally as Show errors).  The same question can't be used twice in a row.  If nobody has gotten anything wrong yet, a random question is used.
* The Shield of Faith question has **20 seconds** (or the question's own time limit if that is shorter, but never less than 10 seconds).  The round ends early once everyone has answered.
* If **at least 2/3 of the connected players** get it right, the **Shield of Faith holds**: the fire bounces off, and the dragon takes 5% of its full health as bonus damage.
* If fewer than 2/3 get it right, the **fire gets through and the dragon heals 10%** of its full health (never above full).
* The reveal shows the correct answer on the projector and on every phone for **4 seconds**, with a count of how many got it right.  This is the moment for the class to learn the question they keep missing.  Then the self-paced questions go on where they left off.

### End of Act 1
* **The dragon is slain** when its health reaches 0.  The time it took is shown later on the results screen.
* If **Battle time runs out**, the dragon isn't killed: it **wakes up and flies away with half of its hoard**.  Act 2 still happens, but every chest pays half (see the loot table).  The game always gets to the team scramble, so the class always gets to play the competitive part.

## Transition (about 8 seconds)
The dragon crashes to the floor (or flies out of the cave).  The pile of gold bursts and coins fly across the projector.  The team banners drop down from the top of the screen in each team's color, and each phone flips to show **"You're on Team ___!"** in that team's color.

## Act 2: The Hoard Scramble (teams)
The projector now shows the gold scattered across the cave, with one pile of gold for each team that grows as they collect.  The **Jackpot** pile sits in the middle.  The Scramble time counts down at the top.

* Everyone keeps answering questions at their own pace.
* **Every correct answer lets the player open 1 of 3 chests** on their phone.  They have **10 seconds** to tap one, or one is opened for them.
* Gold belongs to the **team**.  Everyone's gold goes into the team's pile.

### Loot table
Each chest is rolled when it is opened.  The odds are the same for every chest.

| Chest | Chance | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Coins | 35% | +25 gold for your team |
| Big pile | 20% | +60 gold for your team |
| Lucky charm | 8% | Your next 3 coin chests (Coins or Big pile) pay double |
| Grab 10% | 10% | Take 10% of another team's gold |
| Grab 25% | 4% | Take 25% of another team's gold |
| Dragon's last breath (trap) | 10% | Your team loses 10% of its gold into the Jackpot |
| Dragon scale | 4% | Blocks the next Grab against your team |
| Bats (empty) | 8% | Nothing, just bats |
| Jackpot | 1% | Your team wins the whole Jackpot pile |

* If the dragon flew away in Act 1, **Coins and Big pile pay half** (13 and 30).
* The **Jackpot** starts at 100 gold + 10 per player, and grows with every trap.  The Jackpot chest can't come up in the first 60 seconds of the scramble.  After it's won, it starts again at 0 and keeps growing from traps.

### Grabs (stealing)
* A player who opens a Grab chooses a team from a list that shows every other team's gold.  They have **8 seconds**, or the team with the most gold is picked.
* A team with 0 gold can't be picked.
* A team can only be stolen from **once every 5 seconds**.  If the chosen team is protected right then, the player picks again.
* A **Dragon scale** blocks the next grab against that team.  The projector shows a shield on the team's pile, and the grabbing player sees **"Blocked by a dragon scale!"**.

### Protect from abuse
The same as the other modes: after 2 wrong answers in a row the ✗ stays up 3 seconds longer, and after 3 or more, 4 seconds longer.  Only correct answers open chests.

### End of the game
When the Scramble time is up, **the team with the most gold wins**.  If gold is tied, the team with more correct answers in Act 2 wins.

## Results
The podium shows the winning team, each team's gold, and the class result for Act 1 (**"Dragon slain in 3:42!"** or **"The dragon escaped!"**).

Awards:
* **Dragon slayer**: most hits on the dragon.
* **Faithful Defender**: most Shield of Faith questions right.
* **Master thief**: most gold grabbed from other teams.
* **Most correct answers**.

**Show errors** works the same as in the other modes (every wrong answer counts, including Shield of Faith questions).

The results CSV has one row per player: team, team rank, team gold, correct, wrong, accuracy, dragon hits, Shield of Faith right, Shield of Faith wrong, chests opened, gold found, gold grabbed, gold lost to grabs, gold lost to traps, jackpots won, plus the class's dragon result (slain or escaped), time to slay, Shield of Faith held and Shield of Faith failed.

## Display
### Projector
* **Lobby**: the dragon asleep on its hoard, with the teams in the lobby list.
* **Act 1**: the dragon (in its current stage) on its pile of gold, the shared health bar, the battle clock, and avatars flashing in with sword slashes on each hit.  The flashes are capped so that only the last 12 or so show at a time, so a big class doesn't flood the screen.
* **Fire breath**: the dragon rears up with fire building in its mouth, the Shield of Faith question shows in big text with the answer tiles, and a counter of how many have answered.  Reveal: the shield holds (fire bounces off and the dragon flinches) or the fire gets through (the dragon glows and the health bar fills back up).
* **Act 2**: the scattered gold, a pile and banner for each team with its gold count, the Jackpot pile in the middle, and the scramble clock.  Events pop up from the bottom: "Maya grabbed 120 gold from Team Blue!", "JACKPOT for Team Red!".
* **Podium**: the winning team, the gold totals, the awards, and Show errors / Play again / Done.

### Phones
* **Act 1**: the player's question, with a thin dragon health bar at the top.  After a correct answer, a quick sword slash.
* **Fire breath**: the Shield of Faith question, then Correct/Wrong with the correct answer highlighted, and "Shield held!" or "The fire got through!".
* **Transition**: "You're on Team ___!" in the team's color.
* **Act 2**: the question, then the three chests after a correct answer, then the loot.  For a Grab, the list of teams with their gold.  The team's gold total is always at the top.
* **Podium**: their team's place and their own awards.

## Environment
A deep mountain cave, lit by the glow of the gold and the fire.  The dragon is huge, red and gold, with big horns and glowing eyes.  The art is painted, the same style as the Submarine Squad and Cup of Death art.

## Sound
Music is composed in code like the other modes.
* **Lobby**: a quiet, mysterious cave theme (slow, deep strings, the sound of a dragon breathing).
* **Battle**: a heroic adventure theme (brass, drums, choir).  It gets more intense as the dragon's health goes down and as the battle clock runs out.
* **Fire breath**: a tense rumble that rises during the warning, then war drums under the Shield of Faith question.
* **Scramble**: a fast, playful theme with plucked strings and coin sounds.  It speeds up as the clock runs out.
* **Results**: a victory fanfare.

Sound effects (in sounds.ts): roar, sword hit, fire whoosh, shield clang, coins, chest open, trap hiss, jackpot.

## Decisions
These are the starting choices for this mode.  Every one of them is marked and needs the user to decide.

* Teams are chosen in the lobby (and draggable there) but only matter in Act 2.  Late joiners go to the smallest team.
* The team count can't be more than the number of players, and a team can be a single player.  With one player there is one team and Grabs pay as Coins.
* Small classes (around 3 players) use the same rules.  The numbers to tune after a test game are the minimum dragon health (30), the 2/3 Shield of Faith threshold (2 of 3 players), the grab cooldown and the Jackpot start (100 + 10 per player).
* Dragon health = players × 6 / 10 / 15 hits (Easy / Normal / Hard), with at least 30 in total.  Late joiners add their hits.
* Battle time 3 / 5 / 7 minutes, default 5.  If time runs out, the dragon escapes with half the hoard, and Act 2 still happens with Coins and Big pile paying half.  There is no "game over" in Act 1.
* Fire breath every 30 / 45 / 60 seconds, default 45, and 10 seconds sooner at each wounded stage (never sooner than every 20 seconds).
* The Shield of Faith question is the most-missed question so far, never the same one twice in a row, 20 seconds (10–20), and the shield holds if 2/3 of connected players get it right.  Shield held = 5% bonus damage, shield broken = the dragon heals 10%.
* Scramble time 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 minutes, default 3.
* The loot table and odds above.  There's no Swap chest, because swapping team totals would be too harsh.
* Gold belongs to the team, not to the player.  Act 1 hits don't give any team a head start in Act 2, so every team starts at 0.
* Grabs: 8 seconds to choose, 0-gold teams can't be picked, a team can be stolen from once every 5 seconds, and a Dragon scale blocks one grab.
* Jackpot: starts at 100 + 10 per player, grows from traps, can't come up in the first 60 seconds, and starts again at 0 after it's won.
* 10 seconds to choose a chest, or one is chosen at random.
* Ties: the team with more correct answers in Act 2.
* Awards: Dragon slayer, Faithful Defender, Master thief, Most correct answers.
* The lobby game mode emoji and name: 🐉 Dragon's Hoard.

## Reconnects and late joiners
* A player who reconnects comes back as the same player on the same team, with the same stats, as in every other mode.  A chest choice or Grab that was waiting for them is resolved by the timer while they are gone.
* If the lobby had fewer players than teams when the game started, the team count drops to the number of players.
* A player who joins during Act 1 joins the battle and adds their hits to the dragon's health (the bar keeps the same percentage).  A player who joins during Act 2 goes to the smallest team.
* A player who joins during a Fire breath waits until it is over.
* A disconnected player doesn't count toward the 2/3 needed for the Shield of Faith.

## Bandwidth
* Phones get patches of their own view (view-patch.ts), not full snapshots.
* The dragon's health on the phones is sent in whole percents, so a hit only sends an update when the percent changes, not on every single hit.
* The projector gets the list of recent hits capped at about 12, not every hit.
* In Act 2, the phones only get their own team's gold and, when they open a Grab, the list of team totals.  Projector events are capped to the last few.
* Phones get the smaller phone versions of the art.  The phone only needs the chests, the loot icons and a small dragon head; the big dragon and cave are projector only.
