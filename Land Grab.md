Music should be high energy, "boppy" and has a good beat to it.

The game is timer based, it can last 3, 5, 7 or 10 minutes.  Configurable in the lobby.

This is a team based game that has two modes.  There is the quiz mode and the land claim mode.
There are 2-6 teams (configurable in the lobby).  Each team gets their own color.

The land board is hexagonal tiles that are green like grass.  Each team has a starting location that is marked by a "X" in that teams color.  Each starting location is equidistant from each other.  

A 2 team game should be 10 x 10 tiles.
A 4 team game should be 12 x 12 tiles.
A 6 team game should be 14 x 14 tiles.

### Quiz Mode
During quiz mode, players answer questions as fast as they can.  Every correct answer gets them a claim that can go on a land.  After 3 questions, they get to go into land claim mode.  
### Land Claim Mode
If they have no correct answers, it takes them to the land claim mode and gives them a message that says "You have no tiles to place!", that message lasts 4 seconds, then takes them back into quiz mode.

The players are able to place their colors anywhere on the field except directly on or next to an opposing team's starting point.

If the players are able to surround an area with their claimed land, all tiles that are inside the surrounded area turn that color.  If they are able to surround an enemy starting point, that team is knocked out of the game.
