# Christmas trail-challenge pictures

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. `approved.png` is the 12-picture preview approved by the user; `companion.png` adds 12 matching designs. The four columns are trees, gifts, stockings, and wreaths. Each family now has six distinct pictures. The server shuffles 24 targets without replacement, supporting four instructors with five targets each. Each six-picture player grid contains one target, three other designs from its family, and two pictures from other families.

Runtime assets are the 24 white-backed, 352 × 352 WebP files in `src/client/shared/winter/symbols/` (about 500 KiB total). Re-extract them with `python scripts/winter-art/prepare-symbols.py` (Pillow required). The script only extracts tiles, removes the sheet's empty teal corners, and encodes WebP; it does not redraw the generated art.

Preview the actual phone screens with `bun scripts/winter-art/serve.ts --symbols`, then open http://127.0.0.1:8097/. Query options: `?symbol=0` through `?symbol=23`, `?instructor&symbol=0`, and `?solo`. Player grids support correct and wrong taps. The default chase preview remains available without `--symbols`.

## Approved preview prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: single concept preview contact sheet for Christmas picture-matching symbols in the Sled Squad game's dive/trail-challenge phase. Create a polished landscape sheet with exactly 12 separate white rounded-square tiles arranged as 4 columns by 3 rows on a muted spruce-teal backdrop, generous even gutters. The icons should be large and clear at phone size. Style: charming vintage Christmas gouache illustration, simplified bold silhouettes, crisp dark outlines, restrained painted texture, cranberry red, spruce green, warm ivory, gold. No fine fiddly detail. Each column is a family of three deliberately similar symbols for a listening-and-matching game; variations must be obvious enough to describe by shape or decoration, not just color. Column 1 Christmas trees: top tree with star topper and round baubles, middle tree with bow topper and round baubles, bottom tree with star topper and striped diagonal garland. Column 2 wrapped presents: top square gift with big loop bow and vertical ribbon, middle tall rectangular gift with same bow and ribbon, bottom square gift with diagonal ribbon and holly sprig instead of bow. Column 3 Christmas stockings: top stocking toe pointing right, striped cuff; middle stocking toe pointing left, striped cuff; bottom stocking toe pointing right, fluffy plain cuff and large star patch. Column 4 Christmas wreaths: top evergreen wreath with large bow at bottom and berries; middle same wreath with large bow at top and berries; bottom wreath with bow at bottom and two gold bells hanging in center. Every icon fully contained and centered in its tile, similar apparent visual scale with ample white padding, clean isolated silhouettes. This is a review sheet only, no phone mockup, no UI controls, no headings, no labels, no letters, no watermark. Do not add other objects or extra tiles.
