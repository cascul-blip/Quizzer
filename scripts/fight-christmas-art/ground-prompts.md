# Icing ground image prompts

Tool: built-in image_gen. Initial style reference: output/fight-christmas-concepts/1-gingerbread-siege.png.

## Low

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one transparent 2D side-on game terrain sprite, landscape 5:3 canvas. Reference image is STYLE ONLY: match its rich baked gingerbread earth and especially its thick, glossy white vanilla icing with rounded scalloped dripping lower edges. Show ONLY a continuous cross-section of cookie ground spanning full width and extending solidly to the bottom edge, with ONE rounded central hill and flat platforms on either side. No background, sky, buildings, towers, candy objects, stones floating, characters, trees, text or labels. Transparent sky area above ground. Icing is a generous thick blanket, not a thin outline: about 30 pixels thick at 1000px width, with occasional rounded drips extending 45px down cookie sides, including on flat platforms. A little snowy sugar texture atop but mostly smooth piped icing matching the reference. Hill occupies middle 50% width; outer 25% on each side is flat and level at 90% of canvas height, to support gameplay buildings. Cookie earth is warm honey golden gingerbread with crumbs, subtle cracks and embedded chocolate bits, never grass or rock. Base is rectangular and fills entire bottom edge. Exact flat side view, no perspective. Variant low: broad gentle hill with summit at 65% of canvas height. The overall ground image must fill the width right to both edges, without transparent margins on left/right or at the bottom.
