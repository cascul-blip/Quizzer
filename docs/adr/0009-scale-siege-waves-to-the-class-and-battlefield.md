# Scale Siege waves to the class and battlefield

Kingdom Siege uses answer accuracy to adjust the ordinary wave budget. A wave with no answers gives no evidence about accuracy, so it leaves the scale alone. Each player spends from an individual purse so the class gains no advantage by splitting into players who only answer and players who only build.

Even with the accuracy adjustment, a class can build defenses faster than the waves grow. Compare the board's defense gold against the usual wave budget, including the unspent purses and valuing each defender by the share of its health left. Square the ratio above par because a large board is stronger than its gold alone suggests. Let the boost increase gradually and only after a wave that cost the class no castle hearts. It can fall as soon as the defenses weaken.

Put most of the boost into monster health. Lane entry limits mean that sending more monsters mainly makes them queue. A giant keeps its ordinary wave health scaling and walks and attacks faster under the board boost. The battlefield queues giants separately from the ordinary monster budget and delays their arrival so the horn and lane flash can warn the players.
